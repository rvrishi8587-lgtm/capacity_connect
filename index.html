HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about accessing and using the Capacity Connect learning portal.

Proceed to Login →
How do I get my account approved?

After registration, your account is reviewed according to the applicable role and approval workflow. You can sign in once your account is approved.

Where can I find assessment deadlines?

Assessment information is available inside the relevant course and trainee dashboard. Check your assigned course regularly for upcoming assessments.

Can I track my course progress?

Yes. Your trainee dashboard displays enrolled courses, learning progress and assessment results.

What should I do if I find a technical issue?

Use the Trainee Feedback option from your dashboard to report a technical issue with details so it can be reviewed.

How can I share feedback about a course or trainer?

Logged-in trainees can submit Course/Trainer Feedback through the dedicated feedback form.
