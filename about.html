ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Capacity Connect

MOES & IMD CONTEXT

Strengthening scientific and operational capacity

MoES bridges indigenous scientific research with societal security, supporting the scientific capabilities that underpin critical earth and ocean services.

IMD is the frontline shield for weather forecasting and hazard warnings, where dependable knowledge and continuously trained personnel are essential.

Capacity Connect serves this ecosystem by bridging training gaps and automating skill discovery, helping maintain institutional continuity and making capacity development more measurable.
