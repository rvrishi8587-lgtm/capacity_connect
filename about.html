ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Capacity Connect

A structured digital learning ecosystem for capacity building, training and knowledge development.

THE INSTITUTIONAL CONTEXT

Supporting scientific and technical capacity

Capacity Connect is envisioned as a centralized digital layer for organizing learning resources, training programmes, assessments and learner progress.

MoES

Ministry of Earth Sciences

Supporting the broader capacity-building ecosystem around earth system sciences, services and related technical domains.

IMD

India Meteorological Department

Providing the institutional context for meteorological knowledge, training and professional capacity development.

THE PLATFORM'S MISSION

Solving Problem Statement 26075

Capacity Connect addresses the need for a more organized, accessible and measurable approach to training and capacity development.

CentralizeLearning materials, courses and assessments in one portal.
ConnectBring trainees, trainers and administrators into one workflow.
MeasureTrack progress and assessment outcomes for better visibility.
ImproveCreate a scalable foundation for future digital training services.
